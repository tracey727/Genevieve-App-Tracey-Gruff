## 2. Product definition

GENEVIEVE App™ Dog Parks is a safety, compatibility and journey decision-support system for adult dog owners, travellers and authorised park/council roles in Australia.

Core question: “Is this park suitable for my dog right now?”

The app is built around the real visit lifecycle: Assess → Plan → Arrive → Check In → Supervise → Respond → Check Out. It combines the dog’s current state, intended activity, park rules and facilities, crowd/occupancy context, boundary/gate conditions, weather and heat, environmental hazards, tide/beach access, travel needs and the owner’s ability to supervise.

It is decision support, not a safety guarantee.

It does not diagnose animals or replace veterinary advice.

It does not itself dispatch police, ambulance or veterinary help.

It does not make automatic legal, council-enforcement or liability conclusions.

Official closures, emergency instructions and verified council rules override community reports or favourable calculated conditions.

## 3. Navigation and major app areas

Today — default opening page; immediate dog/park decision, risk, alerts and one-tap status. Full brand header only here.

Parks — Australian park/beach discovery, filters, verified details, maps, boundaries, rules and alternatives.

Journey — separate primary page for before-leaving, door-to-gate planning, Grey Nomad travel, rest stops and saved journeys.

Dogs — profiles, current status, compatibility, personalisation and Best Mate relationships.

More — owner duty, hazards, incidents, Lost & Found, council, accessibility, privacy, account, membership, legal and data functions.

## 4. Today and pre-arrival safety

Always open the app on Today rather than the last screen used.

Select the current dog and intended park/activity.

Run an explainable 1-to-10 park-suitability assessment.

Show score, words, reasons, information source, uncertainty and practical controls.

Display a concise Before You Leave warning.

Check dog condition, equipment, owner readiness, weather/heat, boundaries, gates, space, surface, shade, water, signage and active hazards.

Recommend another park, route or time where conditions are unsuitable or materially uncertain.

Recalculate when arrival conditions differ from the plan.

Truthfully label information as live/current, cached, stale, estimated, community-reported, authorised or council-verified.

## 6. Park, beach, trail and trip-stop discovery

Find nearby Australian dog parks using device location only with permission.

Search throughout Australia by suburb, postcode, place and need.

Find dog beaches, walking tracks/trails where supported, and journey rest-stop candidates.

Show fenced, unfenced and unknown-boundary states without inventing certainty.

Show actual selected places and real map/direction hand-offs.

Calculate distance and route time, or clearly label an estimate if a road route is unavailable.

Carry the selected place into Today, Journey, Arrival and Check-In.

Show data source, freshness, council verification and correction status.

Show sourced off-leash hours, seasonal restrictions, signage, closures and changing local rules.

Do not claim a complete indoor dog-park catalogue until verified.

## 8. Dog profiles, privacy layers and personalisation

Create, edit, select, archive and delete multiple owner-controlled dog profiles.

Public safety layer: owner-selected interaction information such as current status, play style, approach preference, likes, dislikes and triggers.

Behaviour guidance layer: sociability, reactivity, tolerance, energy, play intensity, vulnerability and resource-sharing concerns.

Restricted emergency/health layer: microchip, allergies, medication, vaccination context, weight, vet and emergency contacts.

Record age/life stage, breed or mix, size, sex/desexing status, weight, energy and social comfort without using breed as the behavioural verdict.

Support puppy, senior, anxious, reactive, recovering, disabled and extra-care needs.

Support swimming ability and mobility limitations for coastal personalisation where verified.

Track document dates/reminders without treating owner-entered information as official verification.

Support exact-location hiding, incognito, export, correction and deletion.

## 9. Compatibility and preventative matching

Assess dog-to-dog interaction concern before confrontation rather than simply recording incidents afterward.

Treat pair and group situations separately.

Consider life stage, size, energy, play style, tolerance, reactivity, confidence, vulnerability, resource guarding and current crowd context.

Support gentle-mentor considerations for puppies where appropriate.

Explain the factors that raised/lowered the result and recommend controls.

Do not provide a binary “safe dog/unsafe dog” verdict.

Do not use breed alone as a behavioural verdict.

A favourable result must never be presented as a guarantee.

Recommend another park/time or lower-intensity option when the current combination is unsuitable.

## 10. Best Mates and trusted relationships

Save Best Mate and companion relationships.

Store compatible play preferences and successful interaction context.

Arrival/left alerts require both-owner consent and notification permission.

Relationships can be paused or removed without falsifying historical visits.

A relationship never authorises disclosure of precise location.

After dark, ordinary Best Mate presence alerts are suppressed/delayed by default under Night Safety rules.

## 11. Voluntary check-in, check-out and owner presence

Check In and Check Out are voluntary and privacy-controlled.

My Dog Arrived / I’m Here shortcuts use the same canonical check-in logic.

One active visit per dog.

Idempotency prevents duplicate visits from repeated taps, retries or flaky networks.

Record arrival time, current dog status, owner/session context and departure time.

Save reliably across refresh and approved offline/retry paths.

Expire stale or uncertain presence rather than showing a dog indefinitely.

Incognito and location-sharing controls apply.

Owner Duty supervision timers: 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes.

Owner location states can include inside, at gate, outside or left without publishing a precise GPS trace.

‘I’m still supervising’ renews the timer.

Owner-left state prompts checkout and can open an unattended-dog observation flow.

Unattended reports are observations, not public accusations.

## 12. GPS, boundaries, geofencing and location privacy

The later 12 August specification registers an Ephemeral GPS Boundary Engine (REQ-003). Its purpose is not continuous tracking; it is a short-lived boundary decision with minimal retained location data.

Request device location only in the foreground after an explicit user action.

Do not use continuous/background tracking for ordinary park use, Journey or check-in.

Use the least precise location adequate for an approved boundary check.

Use verified park latitude/longitude and boundary information where available; if only approximate data exists, label it approximate.

After the boundary decision, replace exact coordinates with the minimum derived result required, such as IN-BOUNDARY / OUTSIDE / UNKNOWN plus a coarse park identifier.

Never store exact coordinates in localStorage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches, offline drafts, analytics, URLs, ordinary client/server logs, error messages or crash reports.

Stop location listeners immediately after the approved decision and clear ordinary in-memory references where feasible.

Do not claim browser/phone memory can be physically overwritten with certainty.

Denied permission, stale location, low accuracy or missing boundary data must produce UNKNOWN/restricted behaviour, not a false ‘inside park’ result.

Public precise GPS is prohibited by default.

No unnecessary permanent movement trail.
